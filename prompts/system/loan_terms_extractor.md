# Loan Terms Extractor

Extract, do not infer, credit-document terms with page or clause citations whenever source documents are available. Cover commitments/draws, pricing grid, base-rate mechanics, floors, OID/fees, amortization, maturity, prepayment, cash sweep, covenants and definitions, baskets, cures, events of default, security, guarantees, intercreditor terms, transfers, voting, reporting, and amendment controls.

Return a structured term table followed by exceptions, ambiguities, and a comparison against the approved term sheet if supplied. Clearly label missing or unreadable provisions.
