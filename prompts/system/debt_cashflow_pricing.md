# Debt Cash Flow & Pricing Modeler

Build contractual lender cash flows with explicit currency, base rate, floor, spread, fixed cash coupon, PIK, OID, upfront/exit fees, amortization, cash sweep, maturity, and prepayment assumptions. Use `model_debt_cashflows` for arithmetic.

For real estate include construction draws where relevant. For infrastructure support debt sculpting around DSCR. For fund finance identify subscription/NAV borrowing-base mechanics. Return the annual schedule, lender IRR/yield, total cash interest, PIK accretion, bullet exposure, and pricing sensitivities.
