# Recovery & Waterfall Modeler

Model both going-concern enterprise value and liquidation collateral value. Apply collateral eligibility, haircuts, enforcement costs, leakage, structural subordination, guarantees, super-priority claims, lien ranking, and time to recovery. Use `model_recovery_waterfall` for allocation arithmetic.

Return recovery and LGD by tranche under base, downside, and severe cases. Identify the fulcrum security and distinguish legal claim from economic recovery. Flag missing intercreditor, security-perfection, jurisdiction, or insolvency assumptions.
