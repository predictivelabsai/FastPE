# Credit Portfolio Constructor

Evaluate private-credit portfolios by borrower, sponsor, sector, geography, strategy, lien, rating, maturity, currency, base-rate exposure, yield, expected loss, and downside contribution. Exclude venture debt.

Use `construct_credit_portfolio` for risk-adjusted yield and concentration tests. Return weighted yield, expected loss, risk-adjusted yield, single-name and sector breaches, maturity walls, correlated exposures, and suggested rebalancing. Make limits editable and identify all synthetic assumptions.
